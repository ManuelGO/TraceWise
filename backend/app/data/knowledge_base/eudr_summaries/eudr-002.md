# EUDR Compliance Obligations

## Overview

The EU Deforestation Regulation imposes specific compliance obligations on operators and traders who place covered commodities on the EU market. These obligations form the legal foundation of EUDR and define the actions companies must take.

## Due Diligence System

### Core Requirements

Operators must establish and maintain a due diligence system that:

1. **Identifies and assesses deforestation risk** in their supply chains
2. **Mitigates identified risks** through appropriate measures
3. **Monitors compliance** on an ongoing basis
4. **Maintains documentation** to demonstrate compliance

### Risk Assessment Process

- Map supply chains to country and plot level
- Assess deforestation risk based on:
  - Commodity type and country of origin
  - Historical deforestation rates
  - Regulatory enforcement capacity
  - Third-party certifications

## Traceability Obligations

### Documentation Requirements

Operators must maintain and provide:

- **Proof of origin**: Documentation proving commodity origin
- **Supply chain records**: Complete chain from producer to importer
- **Land identification**: Geolocation data or plots where commodities were produced
- **Legality documentation**: Proof commodity was produced legally

### Record Retention

- Maintain records for minimum 5 years
- Retain all supporting documentation
- Make records available for regulatory inspection
- Ensure data accessibility and verifiability

## Due Diligence Statements

### Content Requirements

Due diligence statements must include:

- **Risk assessment results**: Summary of identified risks
- **Mitigation measures**: Actions taken to address risks
- **Monitoring activities**: Ongoing compliance procedures
- **Verification status**: Proof of due diligence completion

### Submission and Storage

- Statements may be prepared in English
- Stored in paper or electronic format
- Available for competent authorities
- Updated at least annually

## Trader Obligations

### Simplified Requirements

Traders (not placing commodity directly on market) must:

- **Maintain traceability**: Document previous supply chain holder
- **Verify operator status**: Confirm operators have complied
- **Keep records**: Storage of due diligence documentation
- **Provide information**: Share relevant data with authorities

### Verification Responsibilities

- Verify upstream due diligence completion
- Check operator registration status
- Ensure documentation availability
- Facilitate regulatory audits

## Exemptions and Simplified Procedures

### Exempted Commodities

Certain combinations are exempted:
- Derived products (certain processing levels)
- Small quantity thresholds per company

### Simplified Due Diligence

Available for commodities from low-risk countries:
- Reduced documentation requirements
- Streamlined verification procedures
- Based on EUDR risk assessments

## Benchmarking and Risk Assessment

### Country Risk Classification

Based on historical data:
- **Low risk**: <1% deforestation in past 3 years
- **Standard risk**: Between low and high thresholds
- **High risk**: >4% deforestation or governance concerns

### Risk Mitigation Expectations

- Low-risk commodities: Basic documentation
- Standard-risk: Full due diligence required
- High-risk: Enhanced verification and monitoring

## Penalties and Enforcement

### Non-Compliance Consequences

- Administrative fines up to 4% of annual turnover
- Product seizure and destruction
- Import bans
- Criminal prosecution for intentional violations

### Regulatory Controls

- Competent authorities conduct market surveillance
- Inspection authority to verify compliance
- Power to demand documentation
- Ability to suspend operators/traders
